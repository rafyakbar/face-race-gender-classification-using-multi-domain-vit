A direct empirical comparison between the proposed framework and prior studies evaluated on the DemogPairs dataset is summarized in Table~\ref{tab:XII}. The proposed model based on Tri-Domain ViT + SVM (Face $\oplus$ Emotion $\oplus$ Age) achieves an Accuracy of 93.70\%, a Precision of 93.72\%, a Recall of 93.70\%, and an F1-Score of 93.69\%. These quantitative achievements surpass the metrics reported for the Dual-ViT + SVM model (92.41\% Accuracy, 92.38\% F1-Score) and MD-ViT + XGBoost (89.07\% Accuracy, 89.01\% F1-Score) \cite{ref19}, \cite{ref20}. All comparative figures are directly cited from respective publications, serving as contextual positioning on the benchmark dataset rather than an identical experimental replication.

The seminal research on DemogPairs established an essential foundation for quantifying the impact of demographic imbalance on facial recognition systems \cite{ref25}. In response to this challenge, the three-domain representation integration framework proposed in this study achieves the highest accuracy among compared studies on the DemogPairs dataset \cite{ref19}, \cite{ref20}. In addition to evaluating global aggregate metrics, this investigation conducts a comprehensive subgroup disparity analysis on the best tri-domain configuration to transparently map performance variations across demographic subgroups. This detailed evaluation confirms recognition stability across intersectional race and gender categories, though cross-scheme disparity comparison with prior studies was precluded by unrecorded literature metrics \cite{ref19}, \cite{ref20}.
