The granular classification performance profile of the best-performing SVM model based on the tri-domain Face $\oplus$ Emotion $\oplus$ Age fusion is thoroughly evaluated based on OvR metrics as presented in Table~\ref{tab:XI}. All demographic subgroups successfully achieve an F1-Score exceeding 91.00\%, with performance ranges spanning from 91.74\% for Black Females to 96.14\% for White Males, resulting in a range disparity difference of 4.40\% (0.0440). Meanwhile, OvR Accuracy values fall within the range of 97.31\% to 98.70\% with a disparity of 1.39 pp. It should be noted methodologically that the high OvR Accuracy values are also influenced by the 5:1 negative sample dominance ratio in the binary OvR evaluation, such that this metric is not positioned as the sole indicator of performance disparity.

A comparison of subgroup performance profiles between the SVM model and the linear LR classifier under the tri-domain configuration is presented in Table~\ref{tab:XI}. The tri-domain LR model likewise demonstrates high performance consistency, with F1-Score achievements exceeding 91.00\% across all demographic classes, spanning from 91.36\% for Asian Females to 95.58\% for White Males with a range disparity difference of 4.22\% (0.0422). The White Males subgroup consistently records the highest classification performance across both classifiers. These empirical findings demonstrate that the tri-domain representation fusion yields robust visual representations that are stably distributed across all demographic subgroups, in both linear and non-linear models. Nevertheless, this subgroup evaluation reflects granular per-class performance rather than a comprehensive algorithmic fairness audit.
