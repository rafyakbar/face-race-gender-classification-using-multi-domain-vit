\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:evaluation_metrics}

Multi-class classification performance across six demographic subgroups is evaluated at the subgroup level using a binary One-vs-Rest (OvR) scheme \cite{ref44}. This scheme utilizes four per-class confusion matrix components to compute OvR Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-Score for each class $c \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ \cite{ref45}. These components comprise $TP_c$ (True Positive), $TN_c$ (True Negative), $FP_c$ (False Positive), and $FN_c$ (False Negative) \cite{ref46}. OvR Accuracy in \eqref{eq:10} measures the ratio of total correct predictions to the test data, whereas Precision in \eqref{eq:11} and Recall in \eqref{eq:12} map positive precision and detection sensitivity per subgroup \cite{ref47}. The F1-Score in \eqref{eq:13} is the harmonic mean of Precision and Recall for balanced subgroup evaluation \cite{ref47}.
\begin{equation}
\text{Accuracy}_c = \frac{TP_c + TN_c}{TP_c + TN_c + FP_c + FN_c}
\label{eq:10}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{Precision}_c = \frac{TP_c}{TP_c + FP_c}
\label{eq:11}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{Recall}_c = \frac{TP_c}{TP_c + FN_c}
\label{eq:12}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{F1-Score}_c = \frac{2 \cdot \text{Precision}_c \cdot \text{Recall}_c}{\text{Precision}_c + \text{Recall}_c}
\label{eq:13}
\end{equation}

To evaluate overall classification performance on held-out test data, subgroup metrics are aggregated into global performance metrics. In this formulation, $N$ denotes total test samples ($N=2,160$), while $K$ represents the total demographic subgroups ($K=6$). Global Accuracy (Overall Accuracy) in \eqref{eq:14} is calculated as the ratio of total correct predictions, namely the sum of diagonal confusion matrix elements $\sum_{c=1}^K TP_c$, to total test samples $N$.
\begin{equation}
\text{Accuracy}_{\text{Global}} = \frac{\sum_{c=1}^K TP_c}{N}
\label{eq:14}
\end{equation}
Subsequently, global Precision (Macro Precision) in \eqref{eq:15}, global Recall (Macro Recall) in \eqref{eq:16}, and global F1-Score (Macro F1-Score) in \eqref{eq:17} are computed via an unweighted macro average across $K=6$ subgroups to assign equal evaluation weight to each demographic class.
\begin{equation}
\text{Precision}_{\text{Global}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{c=1}^K \text{Precision}_c
\label{eq:15}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{Recall}_{\text{Global}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{c=1}^K \text{Recall}_c
\label{eq:16}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{F1-Score}_{\text{Global}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{c=1}^K \text{F1-Score}_c
\label{eq:17}
\end{equation}
