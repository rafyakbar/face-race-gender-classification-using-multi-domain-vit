Under Bayes' theorem, the GNB classifier estimates class posterior probabilities assuming continuous feature dimensions are conditionally independent \cite{ref35}. A Gaussian probability density function models feature likelihood per target class in \eqref{eq:6}, where $x_i$ is the $i$-th feature, $y$ is the class label, $c$ is the target class, and $\mu_{c,i}$ and $\sigma_{c,i}^2$ are the mean and variance in class $c$ \cite{ref35}:
\begin{equation}
P(x_i \mid y = c) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma_{c,i}^2}} \exp\left(-\frac{(x_i - \mu_{c,i})^2}{2\sigma_{c,i}^2}\right)
\label{eq:6}
\end{equation}

The smoothing parameter var\_smoothing maintains numerical stability against near-zero variance in high-dimensional latent representation spaces \cite{ref36}. The hyperparameter tuning procedure via 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation evaluates combinations of data scaling, PCA dimensionality reduction, and 40 var\_smoothing intervals explored logarithmically from $1.0 \times 10^{-9}$ to $1.0 \times 10^{2}$, yielding 240 grid combinations with a total of 1,200 fits as presented in Table~\ref{tab:IV}.
