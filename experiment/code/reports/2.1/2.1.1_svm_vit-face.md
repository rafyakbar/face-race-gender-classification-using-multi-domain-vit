## 10. Key Observations

- **Best model:** SVM (SVC) with C=10, rbf kernel, degree=2, scale gamma, no PCA, no scaling achieves **90.83% test accuracy**.
- **Class imbalance not an issue:** All 6 classes have exactly 360 test samples (balanced dataset).
- **Strongest class:** Black_Males (F1=0.9366, OvR Accuracy=97.87%).
- **Weakest class:** White_Males (F1=0.8833, OvR Accuracy=96.16%).
- **PCA hurts performance:** Configurations with PCA (bottom 3) drop accuracy by ~17–24 percentage points.
- **Kernel matters:** rbf kernel consistently outperforms linear and poly kernels with PCA combinations.
- **Total training time:** 23 hours 15 minutes 3 seconds (288 combinations × 5 folds + refit).
- **Grid search explored 288 total parameter combinations.**
