## 3. Load Fitur

```python
face_features = joblib.load('features/demogpairs_vit-face.pkl')
age_features = joblib.load('features/demogpairs_vit-age.pkl')
features = {}
for d in tqdm(data):
    key = d['image_path']
    features[key] = np.array(list(face_features[key]) + list(age_features[key]))
print('Jumlah fitur per gambar:', np.array(features[list(features.keys())[0]]).shape[0])
```

## 10. Key Observations

- **Best model:** Logistic Regression with C=0.1, solver=`newton-cg`, max_iter=500, no scaler, no PCA achieves **91.62% test accuracy** (F1 0.9162).
- **Class balance:** All 6 demographic classes have exactly 360 test samples (balanced, 2,160 total).
- **Strongest class:** Black_Males — highest recall 0.9528, F1 0.9436, OvR Accuracy 98.10%.
- **Weakest class:** White_Females — lowest recall 0.8944, F1 0.8982, OvR 96.62%; frequently confused with Asian_Males (13) and Asian_Females (11).
- **Feature concatenation works:** ViT Face + Age (1536-dim) yields 91.62% accuracy, competitive with other LR fusion variants.
- **PCA hurts slightly:** Bottom 4 all use PCA with C=0.01; accuracy drops to 86.41% (~5.2 pts below best). No-PCA configurations dominate top ranks.
- **Solver sensitivity low at best C:** Top 3 share identical CV means (0.9153) across newton-cg with varying max_iter (500/1000/2000); lbfgs (rank 4) is only 0.0001 behind (0.9152).
- **Regularization matters:** C=0.1 consistently tops the table; C=0.01 with PCA forms the floor.
- **Training cost:** 1h 59m wall-clock (jobs) and 22h 58m total sequential time for 270 combinations × 5 folds + refit; per-fold train time ~7–28s depending on solver.
