## 8. Key Observations

1. **Best overall performance:** 85.05% accuracy with GaussianNB, var_smoothing=0.005878, PCA enabled, no scaling.
2. **Strongest class:** Black_Males (precision: 0.8936, recall: 0.8861, F1: 0.8898, OvR accuracy: 0.9634) — the model distinguishes this class most effectively.
3. **Weakest class:** White_Males (precision: 0.7865, F1: 0.8118, OvR accuracy: 0.9352) — lowest precision despite decent recall (0.8389), heavily confused with Black_Females (22 misclassified) and White_Females (17 misclassified).
4. **White_Females** also struggles (recall: 0.8028, F1: 0.8293) — 25 cases misclassified as Asian_Females and 21 as Asian_Males.
5. **Asian classes** perform well (F1: 0.8793 for Asian_Females, 0.8736 for Asian_Males), with high precision and recall.
6. **Black_Females** is the second weakest (F1: 0.8191, OvR accuracy: 0.9403) — 37 cases misclassified as White_Males and 16 as Black_Males.
7. **PCA helps:** Top 3 CV configurations all use PCA (Mean ~0.855), while No-PCA configs drop to ~0.821 and the worst configs (~0.804) all use no PCA + MinMaxScaler + extreme var_smoothing values (27–100).
8. **MinMaxScaler with PCA is suboptimal** — PCA+MinMaxScaler configs cluster around 0.8359 (rank ~80), worse than PCA without scaling (0.8553).
9. **var_smoothing sweet spot:** ~0.003–0.011 yields best CV results; very small (1e-9) or very large (27–100) values degrade performance significantly.
10. **Feature concatenation** of face + emotion + age ViT embeddings (2,304 dims) provides rich representation; PCA was essential for GNB to handle high dimensionality effectively.
11. **Training was moderately expensive** — 1h 4m parallel job time, 7h 23m total including all folds and refit — lighter than SVM/RF tri-domain experiments due to GNB's simplicity.
