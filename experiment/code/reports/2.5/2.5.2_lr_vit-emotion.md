## 10. Key Observations

- **Best model:** Logistic Regression with C=1, saga solver, MinMaxScaler, no PCA achieves **88.47% test accuracy**.
- **Class imbalance not an issue:** All 6 classes have exactly 360 test samples (balanced dataset).
- **Strongest class:** Black_Males (F1=0.9192, OvR Accuracy=97.3%).
- **Weakest class:** White_Males (F1=0.8415, OvR Accuracy=94.6%).
- **PCA hurts performance:** Configurations with PCA (bottom 4) drop accuracy by ~9 percentage points.
- **Total training time:** 10 hours 6 minutes 43 seconds (96 combinations × 5 folds + refit).
- **Grid search explored 270 total parameter combinations** (including train-time variants).
