- **Best vs. worst gap:** 0.9052 vs. 0.8498 accuracy mean — a **5.5 pp** difference across 270 combinations.
- **Dominant factors for best performance:** `C=1`, `pca=None` (no dimensionality reduction), `scaler=MinMaxScaler`.

- **Slowest folds** (top 4): ~22–41 seconds per fold (full 768-d features, C=1 requires more optimization steps).
