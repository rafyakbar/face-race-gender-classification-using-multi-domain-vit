**8. Key Observations**

1. **Best overall performance:** **73.66% accuracy** (F1 0.7354, Precision 0.7363, Recall 0.7366) on 2,160 test samples with `n_estimators=200, max_depth=30, max_features=log2, min_samples_split=2, min_samples_leaf=1, pca=PCA, scaler=None` — mean CV 72.93%, test 73.66% (positive generalization +0.73%).
2. **Weakest single-domain RF:** ViT-Age RF (73.66%) is the lowest among RF single-domain variants (vs vit-face 85.46%, vit-emotion 80.60%), confirming age embeddings are least discriminative for intersectional race-gender via Random Forest.
3. **Strongest classes:** **Asian_Females** (F1 0.7810, Recall 0.7972, OvR 0.9255) and **Black_Males** (F1 0.7800, Recall 0.8028, OvR 0.9245) — easiest to recognize; both have highest OvR accuracy.
4. **Weakest class:** **Black_Females** (F1 0.6786, Recall 0.6333, OvR 0.9000) — 132/360 misclassified, notably as Asian_Females (41) and White_Males (41). Second weakest is White_Females (F1 0.7017, Recall 0.6861).
5. **Confusion patterns:**
   - Asian_Males → White_Males (42) and White_Females (34) — gender-consistent white confusion.
   - Black_Females ↔ Asian_Females (41) and Black_Females → White_Males (41) — ambiguous age-related phenotype boundaries.
   - White_Females → Asian_Males (41) and Asian_Females (30).
   - White_Males → Asian_Males (28) and White_Females (27).
   - Black_Males is most isolated: only 40 errors to Black_Females, minimal cross-gender confusion.
6. **PCA helps strongly:** All top-4 configurations use `pca=PCA` (n_components 0.5 or 0.75), all bottom-4 use `pca=None` — dimensionality reduction from 768-d to PCA components improves RF generalization on ViT-Age, opposite to SVM where PCA sometimes hurt.
7. **No scaler is better:** Best model and all top-4 use `scaler=None`; bottom-2 use `MinMaxScaler` (285,286) — scaling does not help RF on ViT-Age.
8. **max_features=log2 dominates:** All top-4 and bottom-4 use `log2`; `sqrt` never appears in extremes — `log2` is more stable for this 6-class task.
9. **n_estimators 200 > 100:** Top-4 all use 200 trees (~9–10s per fold), bottom-4 all use 100 trees (~5–6s per fold) — doubling trees gains ~9% accuracy.
10. **Training cost:** Grid search 288 combos × 5 CV = 1,440 fits costs **~1h 4m wall-clock (parallel, 60% CPU)** or **~4h 7m sequential** — far cheaper than SVM vit-age (23h sequential) due to RF's faster fitting, but still non-trivial for 768-d features.
