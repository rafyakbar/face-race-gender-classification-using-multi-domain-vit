**8. Key Observations**

1. **Best overall performance:** **85.79% accuracy** (F1 0.8573, Precision 0.8578, Recall 0.8579) on 2,160 test samples with `n_estimators=200, max_depth=None, max_features=sqrt, min_samples_split=2, min_samples_leaf=1, pca=PCA, scaler=None` — mean CV 87.20%, test 85.79% (slight negative generalization gap −1.41% indicates mild overfitting to CV folds, but still strong).
2. **Dual-domain gain:** Face-Age RF (85.79%) outperforms single-domain RF variants: vit-face 85.46% (+0.33%) and vit-age 73.66% (+12.13%) — face dominates, age adds complementary texture/age-morphology cues. Also outperforms GNB face-age 83.15% (+2.64%).
3. **Strongest classes:** **Black_Males** (F1 0.9064, Recall 0.9278, OvR 0.9681) and **Asian_Males** (F1 0.8861, OvR 0.9620) — highest discriminability, especially Black_Males with near-perfect recall.
4. **Weakest class:** **White_Females** (F1 0.8158, Recall 0.7750, OvR 0.9417) — 81/360 misclassified, notably as Asian_Females (32) and Asian_Males (21). Second weakest is White_Males (F1 0.8252) — confuses with Black_Females (26) and White_Females (21).
5. **Confusion patterns:**
   - White_Females → Asian_Females (32) and Asian_Males (21) — cross-race gender confusion within females.
   - Black_Females → White_Males (23) and Black_Males (21) — female confusion across race to male counterpart.
   - White_Males → Black_Females (26) and White_Females (21) — male confusion to female within white/other race.
   - Asian_Males → White_Males (21) and White_Females (12).
   - Black_Males is most distinct: only 26 errors total (12 to Black_Females, 7 to Asian_Females), minimal leakage.
6. **PCA consistently helps:** All top-4 and bottom-4 configurations use `pca=PCA` — PCA on 1,536-d face-age embedding improves generalization; no top config uses `pca=None`. RF benefits from reduced dimensionality where SVM also benefited.
7. **No scaler is key for top:** All top-4 use `scaler=None`; all bottom-4 use `scaler=MinMaxScaler` — MinMax scaling slightly degrades RF performance on concatenated ViT embeddings (penurunan ~1.45% accuracy).
8. **max_features nuance:** Best model uses `sqrt` (top-1), but 2 of top-4 use `sqrt` and 1 uses `log2`; bottom-4 all use `log2` — `sqrt` is slightly superior for face-age RF.
9. **n_estimators 200 dominates:** All top-4 use 200 trees (~17–19s per fold), all bottom-4 use 100 trees (~9–10s per fold) — doubling trees gives +1.45% accuracy at ~2× training cost.
10. **Training cost:** Grid search 288 combos × 5 CV = 1,440 fits costs **~1h 45m wall-clock (parallel, 60% CPU, 6,311s)** or **~6h 47m sequential (24,456s)** — moderate vs SVM face-age (8.1h sequential) and far cheaper than SVM tri-domain.
