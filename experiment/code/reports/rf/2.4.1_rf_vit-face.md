4. **White_Males** also struggles (F1: 0.8201), confused with Black_Females (28 misclassified) and White_Females (23 misclassified).

7. **PCA + MinMaxScaler** was selected as the best preprocessing combination, suggesting dimensionality reduction helped Random Forest handle the 768-dim ViT-face space while scaling normalized the embedding range.
