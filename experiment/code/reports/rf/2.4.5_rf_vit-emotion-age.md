## 7. Key Observations

1. **Best overall performance: 81.11% accuracy** with 200 estimators, max_depth=None, log2 max_features, min_samples_split=5, min_samples_leaf=2, PCA enabled, no scaling — confirming that dimensionality reduction helps for the 1536-dim emotion+age space.
2. **Weakest combination without PCA or with shallow trees performs worst** (~72.69% CV mean for max_depth=20, 100 trees) — depth matters more than scaling.
3. **Strongest class: Asian_Females** (precision 0.8497, recall 0.8639, F1 0.8567, OvR accuracy 0.9519) — emotion+age ViT still captures distinctive features well for this group.
4. **Weakest class: White_Males** (precision 0.7732, recall 0.7861, F1 0.7796) — confused notably with Black_Females (27), Asian_Males (26), and White_Females (16).
5. **Black_Females struggles most on recall** (0.7556, F1 0.7716) — misclassified as Asian_Females (28), White_Males (32), and Black_Males (24).
6. **Black_Males has highest recall** (0.8694) despite moderate precision (0.8088) — model is sensitive to this class but with some false positives from neighboring classes.
7. **Emotion+Age alone (1536 dims) is weaker than Face-inclusive features** — compare 81.11% here vs 86.20% for face+emotion+age (2,304 dims) and vs face-only baselines; age+emotion captures demographic signal but less than face identity embeddings.
8. **Scaling (MinMaxScaler) not beneficial** — best config uses `scaler=None`; results with MinMaxScaler rank lower in CV table.
9. **PCA consistently in top ranks** — all top-4 CV configurations use `pca='PCA'`, suggesting noise reduction from concatenated emotion+age space is critical.
10. **Training was moderately expensive** — 6h 42m total compute (1h 44m wall time with ~60% CPU parallelism) for 288 combinations × 5 folds.
