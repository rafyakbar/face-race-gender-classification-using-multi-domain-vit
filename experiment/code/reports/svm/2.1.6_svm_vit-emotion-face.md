## 8. Key Observations

1. **Best overall performance:** 93.29% accuracy with C=10, rbf kernel, gamma=scale, degree=2, MinMaxScaler, no PCA — the highest among all ViT single- and dual-domain SVM experiments.
2. **Dual-domain gain:** 1,536-dim concatenated features (emotion + face) outperform single-domain ViT-Face (~91.5%), ViT-Emotion (~83.5%), and single-domain ViT-Age (87.64%), showing complementary value of emotion and face embeddings.
3. **Strongest class:** Black_Males (recall: 0.967, F1: 0.959, OvR accuracy: 0.986) — only 12 misclassifications total, confirming the model distinguishes this class most effectively.
4. **Weakest class:** White_Males (F1: 0.907, OvR: 0.969) — 32 errors, most confused with Black_Females (14 cases), Asian_Males (9), and White_Females (8).
5. **Asian_Females excels in precision** (0.977, highest) but 9 cases are misclassified as Black_Females and 8 as White_Females, 6 as Black_Males.
6. **White_Females** confusion is dominated by Asian_Males (14 cases), mirroring cross-race gender confusion seen in ViT-Age experiments.
7. **Black_Females vs White_Males cross-confusion:** 14 White_Males predicted as Black_Females and 11 Black_Females predicted as White_Males — the largest off-diagonal pair.
8. **PCA consistently degrades performance** — all bottom-3 configs use PCA (n_components=0.75) with poly kernel and low C (0.01–0.1), dropping to ~71–72% CV accuracy.
9. **Degree has negligible effect for RBF top configs** — degree 2 and 3 with identical rbf/MinMaxScaler/C=10 both achieve 0.9231 mean CV accuracy (rank 1 and 2 tied).
10. **Training was extremely expensive** — 50h 35m total (6h 14m parallel job time) for 288 combinations × 5-fold CV, reflecting the 1,536-dim feature space and 8,640 training samples.
