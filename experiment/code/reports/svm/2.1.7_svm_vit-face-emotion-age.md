**8. Key Observations**

1. **Best overall SVM:** **93.70% test accuracy** (F1 0.9369) with `SVC(C=10, kernel=poly, degree=2, gamma=scale, pca=None, scaler=None)` — the highest among all ViT single-, dual-, and tri-domain SVM experiments in this series (compare: ViT-Face ~90.8%, ViT-Emotion ~90.2%, ViT-Face+Emotion 93.29%).
2. **Tri-domain complementarity:** 2,304-dim concatenation (Face + Emotion + Age) outperforms any mono- or bi-domain concatenation, confirming that age, emotion, and face ViT embeddings carry non-redundant demographic cues.
3. **Poly kernel wins in tri-domain:** Unlike single-domain ViT experiments dominated by `rbf`, the top tri-domain config uses `poly` degree 2 — indicating useful non-linear polynomial interactions across the three embedding spaces.
4. **No scaling / no PCA needed at top:** Top-3 all have `pca=None`; best has `scaler=None`. MinMaxScaler only appears from rank ~96 downward; PCA degrades the best poly/rbf configs, confirming ViT features are already well-normalized and high-dimensional structure is informative.
5. **Strongest class:** `Black_Males` (F1 0.9614, Recall 0.9694, OvR Accuracy 0.9870) — only 11 errors (4→Asian_Females, 2→Asian_Males, 4→Black_Females, 1→White_Males).
6. **Weakest class:** `White_Females` (Recall 0.8944, F1 0.9174, OvR 0.9731) — 38 errors, dominant confusion is 16→Asian_Males and 9→White_Males, plus 6→Black_Females and 5→Asian_Females.
7. **Confusion patterns:** Largest off-diagonal pairs are `White_Females→Asian_Males` (16), `White_Males→Black_Females` (9) and `Asian_Females→Black_Females` (9), mirroring cross-race gender confusion seen in other ViT experiments.
8. **Asymmetric errors:** `Asian_Females` is the most precisely predicted class (Precision 0.9549, OvR 0.9829) but still confuses 9→Black_Females and 8→White_Females; `Asian_Males` has near-perfect recall (0.9472) with only 1→Asian_Females.
9. **Hyperparameter sensitivity:** High `C=10` dominates top-3; low `C` (0.01–0.1) with `linear` or `poly+PCA+MinMaxScaler` drops CV mean to ~0.88–0.90 (mid) and collapses to 0.5556 (bottom) — underscoring that regularization and kernel choice dominate.
10. **Training cost is highest in the SVM series:** 42,627 s wall-clock (11h 50m) and 269,793 s sequential (3d 2h 56m) for 288×5 fits on 8,640 samples in 2,304 dims — reflecting cubic scaling of kernel SVM with feature dimension and sample count.
