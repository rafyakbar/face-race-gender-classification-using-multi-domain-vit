The SVM classifier constructs a maximum margin hyperplane to separate the six intersectional classes in the high-dimensional latent space~\cite{ref40}. Kernel functions project the nonlinear distribution of multi-domain features into a higher-dimensional space, encompassing linear, Radial Basis Function (RBF), and polynomial kernels~\cite{ref41}. The polynomial kernel formulation in \eqref{eq:11} computes the dot product $\langle \mathbf{x}_i, \mathbf{x}_j \rangle$ between feature vectors $\mathbf{x}_i$ and $\mathbf{x}_j$, where $\gamma$ denotes the kernel scale, $d \in \{2, 3\}$ determines the mapping degree, and the intercept constant $\text{coef0} = 0.0$ follows the scikit-learn default value~\cite{ref42}:
\begin{equation}
K(\mathbf{x}_i, \mathbf{x}_j) = (\gamma \langle \mathbf{x}_i, \mathbf{x}_j \rangle + \text{coef0})^d, \quad d \in \{2, 3\}
\label{eq:11}
\end{equation}
This polynomial mapping facilitates the efficient capture of nonlinear interactions across visual dimensions~\cite{ref43}. Hyperparameter optimization evaluates the penalty parameter $C \in \{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10\}$, kernel variations, kernel coefficient $\gamma$, polynomial degrees, as well as scaling and PCA options, yielding 288 Cartesian grid combinations (108 effective unique configurations) or 1,440 total fits under 5-Fold StratifiedGroupKFold CV with subject isolation as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:6}.
