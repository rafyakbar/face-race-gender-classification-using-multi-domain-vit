\subsection{Classification Pipeline and Leakage-Free Protocol}
\label{sec:classification_pipeline}
\label{sec:classification_pipeline_and_strategy}

The modular pipeline integrates feature scaling (None, MinMaxScaler), PCA dimensionality reduction (None, 50\%, 75\%), and downstream classifiers into a unified processing chain. The transformation chain is modeled in \eqref{eq:12}, where the input vector $\mathbf{x}$ is scaled to $\tilde{\mathbf{x}}$, reduced via PCA projection to $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$, and then mapped to the estimated label $\hat{y} \in \{0, \dots, K-1\}$ for the $K = 6$ intersectional subgroups. To guarantee zero identity leakage and zero statistical leakage, scaling and PCA are strictly fitted only on the training folds of the 5-Fold StratifiedGroupKFold GridSearchCV on the training subset (480 subjects, 8,640 images) with groups = subject\_id isolation. The hyperparameter search reconciles (SVM: 288 + LR: 96 + RF: 288 + GNB: 240) = 912 hyperparameter combinations per feature scheme $\times$ 5 folds = 4,560 fits per feature scheme $\times$ 7 feature schemes + 28 final refits on the training subset prior to held-out evaluation.

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{x} \xrightarrow{\text{Scaler}} \tilde{\mathbf{x}} \xrightarrow{\text{PCA}} \hat{\mathbf{x}} \xrightarrow{\text{Classifier}} \hat{y} \in \{0, \dots, K-1\}
\label{eq:12}
\end{equation}
