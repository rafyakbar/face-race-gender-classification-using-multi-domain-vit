\begin{abstract}
Intersectional facial demographic attribute recognition faces challenges from affective expression variations, biological aging, inter-subgroup visual similarity, limitations of single-domain representations, and identity leakage risks caused by random image splitting. This study proposes a multi-domain Vision Transformer (ViT) fusion framework that integrates facial biometric, emotional expression, and biological age representations through 2,304-dimensional concatenation, coupled with feature scaling, Principal Component Analysis, and the optimization of four classical machine learning classifiers to recognize six intersectional classes from three macro-racial groups (Asian, Black, White) and two genders on the DemogPairs benchmark. The evaluation employs an identity-leakage-free Subject-Disjoint primary protocol alongside an image-based Stratified Split as a data leakage ablation study. Under the primary Subject-Disjoint protocol, Logistic Regression leads performance with an accuracy of 88.66\%, macro precision of 88.75\%, macro recall of 88.66\%, and macro F1-score of 88.68\%, inferentially equivalent to Support Vector Machine with an accuracy of 88.61\%, macro precision of 88.71\%, macro recall of 88.61\%, and macro F1-score of 88.63\%. McNemar's test demonstrates the superiority of tri-domain fusion over single-domain configurations ($p < 0.001$) and confirms predictive parity between Logistic Regression and Support Vector Machine ($p = 1.0000$). Conversely, in the Stratified Split ablation study, identity overlap yields pseudo-optimistic performance estimates, where Support Vector Machine and Logistic Regression achieve an accuracy of 93.70\% and macro F1-score of 93.69\%, and an accuracy of 92.73\% and macro F1-score of 92.73\%, respectively. Algorithmic fairness evaluation of the top-performing model reveals cross-subgroup prediction stability, with recall ranging from 84.72\% to 94.44\% and an Equal Opportunity Difference gap of 9.72 percentage points.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
Biometrics, face recognition, feature extraction, intersectional demographic recognition, multi-domain feature fusion, Vision Transformer.
\end{keywords}
