\begin{abstract}
Intersectional facial demographic attribute recognition faces challenges from affective expression variations, biological aging, inter-subgroup visual similarity, single-domain representation limitations, and identity leakage risks caused by random image splitting. This study proposes a multi-domain Vision Transformer (ViT) fusion framework that integrates facial biometric, emotional expression, and biological age representations through 2,304-dimensional concatenation, coupled with feature scaling, Principal Component Analysis, and optimization of four classical machine learning classifiers to recognize six intersectional classes from three macro-racial groups (Asian, Black, White) and two genders on the DemogPairs benchmark. The evaluation employs an identity-leakage-free Subject-Disjoint primary protocol alongside an image-based Stratified Split protocol as a data leakage ablation study. Under the primary Subject-Disjoint protocol, Logistic Regression leads performance with 88.66\% accuracy, 88.75\% macro precision, 88.66\% macro recall, and 88.68\% macro F1-score, inferentially equivalent to Support Vector Machine achieving 88.61\% accuracy, 88.71\% macro precision, 88.61\% macro recall, and 88.63\% macro F1-score. McNemar's test demonstrates the superiority of tri-domain fusion over single-domain configurations ($p < 0.001$) and confirms predictive parity between Logistic Regression and Support Vector Machine ($p = 1.0000$). Conversely, in the Stratified Split ablation study, identity overlap yields pseudo-optimistic performance estimates, where Support Vector Machine and Logistic Regression record 93.70\% accuracy and 93.69\% macro F1-score, and 92.73\% accuracy and 92.73\% macro F1-score, respectively. Algorithmic fairness evaluation of the top-performing model shows cross-subgroup prediction stability, with recall ranging from 84.72\% to 94.44\% and an Equal Opportunity Difference gap of 9.72 percentage points.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
Biometrics, face recognition, feature extraction, intersectional demographic recognition, multi-domain feature fusion, Vision Transformer.
