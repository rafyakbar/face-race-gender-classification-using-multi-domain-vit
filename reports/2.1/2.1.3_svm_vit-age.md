## 8. Key Observations

1. **Best overall performance:** 87.64% accuracy with C=10, rbf kernel, gamma=scale, no scaler, no PCA.
2. **Strongest class:** Black_Males (recall: 0.911, F1: 0.919, OvR accuracy: 0.973) — the model distinguishes this class most effectively.
3. **Weakest class:** White_Females (recall: 0.836, F1: 0.830, OvR accuracy: 0.943) — the most confusion occurs here, especially misclassified as Asian_Males (25 cases).
4. **Asian classes** perform well (F1: 0.898 for Asian_Females, 0.875 for Asian_Males), with Asian_Females achieving the second-highest recall (0.908).
5. **White_Males** confusion: 19 cases misclassified as Black_Females, 8 as Asian_Males, and 15 as Black_Females.
6. **Black_Males** shows some confusion with Asian_Females (14 misclassified) and Black_Females (13 misclassified).
7. **PCA configurations** performed worse — best PCA config reached only ~80% CV accuracy (rank ~96), while no-PCA configs dominated the top positions.
8. **Scaling with MinMaxScaler** showed minimal benefit for RBF kernels (ranks 1 vs 3 are nearly identical).
9. **Linear and poly kernels** with low C values and PCA performed worst (as low as ~32% accuracy), indicating the 768-dim age features need non-linear separation.
10. **Training was very computationally expensive** — over 35 hours total for the full grid search with 5-fold CV across 288 parameter combinations (4h 15m for parallel jobs, 35h 20m total including refit).
